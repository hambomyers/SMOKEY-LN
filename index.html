Property Description

Nine-lot mobile home community in Elkton, Virginia, Rockingham County. Two contiguous parcels (130E-(2)-L21 and 130E-(2)-L22) totaling 1.755 acres with road frontage on Smokey Lane off Rockingham Pike. Park has operated continuously as a mobile home community since the early 1970s.

Seven lots currently rented. One lot (161) is a vacant pad. One lot (143) houses the park's water pump station and is not a revenue lot. Six of the seven paying tenants own their trailers; the seller collects lot rent only. One lot (163) is rented with the trailer included.

Environmental

A Phase I / Limited Phase II Environmental Site Assessment was completed by HydroGeo Environmental, LLC in April 2022. Report and Phase II soil and groundwater analytical results available under NDA. HydroGeo's stated conclusion: no further environmental investigation recommended.

Infrastructure

Septic system has been updated by current ownership. Risers installed to provide inspection access to both the septic tank and pump tank.

Water service is provided by the on-site well and pump house at lot 143.
